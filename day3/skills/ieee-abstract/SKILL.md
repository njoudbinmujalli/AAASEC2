# IEEE-Style Abstract

When asked to write an IEEE-style abstract, ALWAYS follow this structure, as ONE continuous paragraph (no headers, no bullet points):

1. **Context/Problem** — 1-2 sentences: what gap or problem does this work address?
2. **Method/Approach** — 1-2 sentences: what was actually done (technique, architecture, dataset, or system)?
3. **Results** — 1-2 sentences: what was found, with concrete metrics or numbers where possible.
4. **Significance** — 1 sentence: why this matters, or what it enables.

Rules:
- Total length between 150-250 words.
- Write in third person / passive academic style ("we propose", "this paper presents") — never first person "I".
- No citations, no footnotes, no in-text references.
- No unexplained acronyms on first use — spell out then abbreviate.
- Past tense for completed work, present tense for what the paper generally presents.
- Output as a single unbroken paragraph, not a list.
